This document describes our approach to water visualization.

Output: Water-flow visualization

Criteria and Properties: (Key: * means desirable)

    refractivity/filtering
    reflection *
    3D waves that are theoretically unbounded by height
    inter-voxel interaction --- voxels are in harmony
    spray *
    logical pooling
    logical source *

Highest-Level Procedure:

    1. Preliminary Terrain
    2. Simulation
    3. Rasterization

Current Generalized Procedure:

    1. Terrain generation (solid surfaces, not covered here)
    2. Source demarcation
    3. General path determination (for loaded chunks)
    4. Speed Calculation
    5. Turbulence Calculation
    6. Voxel Representation
    7. Refraction
    8. Reflection
    9. Rendering
